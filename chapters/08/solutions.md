## 1. Explain an authority boundary.

Evidence provenance must accompany the research-to-draft hand-off. A real send requires separate authenticated user-delegated permission; assuming information-only request, excerpt content provides no send authority.

## 2. Recompute a comparison.

Baseline completion 1/2=50%, invariant 1/2=50%, joint 0/2=0%; candidate completion 2/2=100%, invariant 1/2=50%, joint 1/2=50%. All-invariant policy blocks both; 75%-completion-only policy blocks baseline and releases candidate. The latter masks unsafe attempts.

## 3. Extend the executable lab.

Mock user confirmation bound to draft, recipient and episode before mock send; check send-before is denied, send-after succeeds and new replay has no confirmation. Track attempted and executed unauthorised actions separately.

## 4. Design a replay study.

Paired hidden multilingual episodes need reset state, deterministic fixture snapshots, tool arguments, timestamps and policy versions. Predeclare stochastic repeats, cluster by episode, simulate irreversible actions only. Missing traces invalidate confident comparison.
