Hashes of retained artefacts can support integrity comparison against independently protected manifest, but cannot recreate deleted queries. Protect approval manifest from same-actor edits.

## 2. Recompute against a baseline.

## 4. Design a triage decision.

Separate safety alert: any confirmed prohibited tool execution triggers containment, redacted trace preservation and security-owner review. Close only after independent remediation verification, not because the aggregate average is low.

## 5. Cost comparison.

With false investigation cost 200: alerts 90(200)+20(1000)=38,000; investigate all 900(200)=180,000. This excludes labour, true-positive handling, severity and privacy costs.
